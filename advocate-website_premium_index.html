About the Advocate

Adv. Manibhushan Kumar

With over 14 years of courtroom litigation experience, Adv. Manibhushan Kumar represents clients before the Supreme Court of India, the High Court of Delhi, and the District Courts at Rohini and Saket.

His practice rests on thorough case preparation, sound legal strategy and honest, timely communication. Whether the matter is a trial-court dispute or a complex appellate proceeding, every client receives focused and personal attention.

He adheres strictly to the professional standards of the Bar Council of India, placing integrity, confidentiality and ethical practice above all else.
